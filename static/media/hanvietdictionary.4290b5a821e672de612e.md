# Han-Viet Dictionary

## Background

This was my first app published on the iOS app store! I am super proud because besides this website, I've never put something out into the world for people to use before. And for it to be on something I am very passionate about, languages, is very exciting. So I wanted to tell you a little about what this application is about.

They say language is the bedrock of culture. And while many people across the world are proud of their language and what it represents about their culture, few often look beyond their borders to see commonalities with people outside. This is especially true in East Asia, where despite how different they sound and where they came from, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, and Vietnamese all share one thing: Han Chinese characters and vocabularly. 

### Private Policy
HanViet Dictionary does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All lookups are performed entirely on-device using a local dataset.
