# My time in the Dominican Republic (2023)

This is the first of my reflections on my travels over the past few years. In 2023, I got a chance to visit a good friend of mine in the Dominican Republic (DR). I was really excited because I got a chance to visit the land I had heard so much about throughout college. I remember the first time I met this friend, my first week of college, he was the center of attention in the lounge of our dorm showing off his dance moves and swinging to songs from his home country. I had heard from other Americans that it was a popular tourist destination spot, but meeting Joel for the first time really put the DR on the map for me.

In 2016, I went on a trip with my high school choir to Puerto Rico. This was my first time visiting an island in the carribean. I was particularly strucked by how beautiful the nature was, but since Puerto Rico is a US territory, I still felt a sense of familiarity being on the island. And while I had taken ~4 years of Spanish at that point, my interest in the language was limited to truly appreciate the culture and what it had to offer.

In contrast, despite the DR being actually closer to the US homeland than Peurto Rico, it felt completely foreign to me the first time I touched down in Las Americás airport and the moment I inhaled the outside air. I felt particulary nostalgic because the air smelled like Vietnam, which up to this point I had not been back since 2007, a faint scent of hot gasoline in a tropical setting that solidified for me how different this country was despite it only being a two hour flight from Miami. However, for me flying from Los Angeles, it took much longer with two stops in Monterrey and Mexico City on the way. Being my first international flight since I was younger, I was absolutely baffled by how large Earth is.

One particulary important thing to note is that beginning in 2022 my interest in languages first blossomed. I set a lofty goal for myself driven by a desire to connect with others across the world to master five languages in my life: English (native), Vietnamese (heritage), Spanish (high school), Mandarin Chinese, and Arabic. Each language representing a major region of the world. I started off with relearning Spanish since I already had the fundamentals. And with Lionel Messi and Argentina winning the World Cup in 2022, I was inspired to improve my Español. I was hoping this trip would help facilitate this endeavor. However, despite the few instances I heard Joel speak Spanish and watching his YouTube videos, I was in for a whirlwind of surprise by how uniquely distinct Dominican Spanish is.
